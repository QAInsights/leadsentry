WHAT WE GIVE YOU:

APIs and an MCP server for building applications for the physical world. Any US address or coordinate, hundreds of cited facts about it — terrain, hazards, power, water, soil, buildings, land. Every value comes back with its source and a timestamp, so what you build can show its work.

BUILD AN AGENT:

This is the one hard rule. We want agents — things that reason, decide, and act on physical-world data. Not a website with a map on it.

WHAT WE'RE JUDGING:

1. What did you combine us with? Court filings, permit databases, satellite imagery, ship tracking, eBird, spot GPU pricing, tax-delinquency rolls, local news. Mireye is one input — the interesting part is what sits next to it.

2. Is it a real problem? Someone loses money, time or health today because this doesn't exist.

3. Who writes the cheque? "Developers might like this" is not a buyer.

JUST SOME COMPLEX IDEAS (dont build these):

Eagle-strike screening for wind developers. We know where golden eagles nest. Developers pay consultants five figures to learn this too late.

Foundation forensics. Shrink-swell soils and bedrock depth against building age — why houses on one street crack and the next street doesn't.

Which way the smell blows. Prevailing wind against hazardous facilities, crossed with rental listings. Renters can't check this today.

Heat that kills. Wet-bulb temperature, tree canopy and housing density, crossed with outage data. Cities are funded to answer this and can't.

What's listed under your dirt. Endangered species with critical habitat on a parcel — the thing that blocks a project after you've bought it.

Carbon-aware compute routing. Grid carbon intensity, power price and fibre availability against spot GPU prices.

Is the river fishable today. Live federal gauge flow, crossed with guides and weather.
